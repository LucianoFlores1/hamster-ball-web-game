## Title
ZipZapp

## Short description
ZipZapp is a one-tap neon arcade game. Dodge blocks at full speed, switch lanes at the very
last second to graze them, build your multiplier up to x8 and chase your high score to a
pumping synthwave soundtrack.

## Controls
- Tap / Click / Space: switch lane
- Arrow keys or A / D: go to the left / right lane
- Esc or P: pause
- M: mute

## How to play
Your ball zips down a two-lane neon highway while blocks drop from the sky. Tap to switch lanes
and dodge them. The later you dodge, the better: a last-second dodge is a graze, gives bonus
points and raises your multiplier (x2, x3, x4, x6, x8). Go 3 seconds without a graze and the
multiplier resets. The road keeps getting faster. How far can you go?
