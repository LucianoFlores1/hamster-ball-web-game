## Title
Carril Neon

## Short description
Dodge neon blocks at full speed with one tap. Switch lanes at the last moment to graze the
blocks, build your multiplier up to x8 and chase your high score in a synthwave world.

## Controls
- Tap / Click / Space: switch lane
- Arrow keys or A / D: go to the left / right lane
- Esc or P: pause
- M: mute

## How to play
Your ball races down a two-lane neon highway. Blocks drop from the sky into either lane.
Switch lanes to avoid them. The later you switch, the better: a last-second dodge counts as
a graze, gives bonus points and raises your multiplier (x2, x3, x4, x6, x8). Go 3 seconds
without a graze and the multiplier resets. The road keeps getting faster. How far can you go?
